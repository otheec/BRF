Flavor Profile

A masterclass in the New England style, this hazy powerhouse explodes with vibrant notes of citrus and tropical fruit. A subtle backbone of pine provides a clean, resinous finish that balances the initial sweetness. Remarkable for its smooth, pillowy texture that lingers on the palate.

Grapefruit Mango Resinous Creamy

Tasting Notes

air

Aroma

Heavy scents of overripe peach, tangerine zest, and a hint of fresh damp pine needles.

visibility

Appearance

Opaque, straw-yellow body with a persistent, creamy two-finger head of tight white bubbles.

texture

Mouthfeel

Full-bodied and velvety with soft carbonation. Minimal bitterness despite the high hop load.

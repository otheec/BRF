Our Story

Founded in the heart of the Blue Ridge Mountains, Wildwood Brewing Co. began with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between traditional old-world brewing techniques and the bold, experimental spirit of American craft beer.

What started in a small garage in 2014 has grown into a community staple. We specialize in forest-foraged adjuncts, wild fermentation, and hop-forward ales that reflect the rugged beauty of North Carolina's wilderness.
